# Anti-Money Laundering and KYC Requirements (Sample)

> Synthetic document created for demonstration purposes in this project. It does not
> represent an official text from any financial intelligence unit, central bank, or
> regulatory body.

## 1. Customer Identification (KYC)

Every financial institution must collect, at minimum, full name, tax ID (individual or
corporate), date of birth or incorporation, address, and the purpose of the business
relationship before onboarding a customer. For legal entities, identifying the ultimate
beneficial owners holding 25% or more of corporate ownership is mandatory.

## 2. Transaction Monitoring

Institutions must maintain monitoring systems capable of identifying atypical transaction
patterns, including: structuring transactions to avoid reporting thresholds, activity
inconsistent with the customer's declared profile, and transactions with counterparties in
high-risk jurisdictions.

## 3. Suspicious Activity Reporting

Transactions identified as suspicious must be reported to the competent regulatory
authority within 24 hours of completing the internal analysis, without prior notice to the
customer involved (no "tipping-off").

## 4. Record Retention

Customer identification and transaction records must be kept for at least 5 years after the
end of the business relationship or the completion of the transaction, whichever occurs
last.

## 5. Risk Classification

Customers must be classified into risk categories (low, medium, high) based on criteria
such as expected transaction volume, declared economic activity, and the jurisdiction of
origin of funds. Customers classified as high risk require Enhanced Due Diligence.
