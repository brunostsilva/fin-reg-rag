# Open Finance Participation Requirements (Sample)

> Synthetic document created for demonstration purposes in this project. It does not
> represent an official text from the Central Bank of Brazil or any regulatory body.

## 1. Participant Types

The Open Finance ecosystem classifies participants into two types:

- **Data holders (transmitters)**: institutions that share customer data upon consent.
- **Data receivers**: institutions that receive shared data to offer financial products
  and services.

A single institution may act as both a data holder and a data receiver.

## 2. Customer Consent

Every data-sharing event depends on the customer's explicit consent, valid for a maximum
of 12 months, renewable through a new consent action. The customer may revoke consent at
any time, with immediate effect on future sharing.

## 3. Shareable Data Scope

Eligible data for sharing includes: registration data, checking and savings account
transaction data, credit operation data, and investment data. Sensitive data, such as
passwords and authentication information, is never shared between institutions.

## 4. Security and Certification

Every participating institution must hold a digital certificate issued by a Certificate
Authority recognized by the Open Finance governance structure, and must implement secure
API communication following the FAPI (Financial-grade API) standard.

## 5. Incident Liability

In the event of a security incident resulting in a leak of shared data, the institution
responsible for the incident must notify the Open Finance governance structure within 24
hours and affected customers within 72 hours.
