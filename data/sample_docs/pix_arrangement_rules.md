# Instant Payment Arrangement Rules (Sample)

> Synthetic document created for demonstration purposes in this project. It does not
> represent an official text from the Central Bank of Brazil or any regulatory body.
> It is loosely inspired by Brazil's Pix instant payment scheme to provide a realistic,
> domain-relevant corpus for the RAG pipeline.

## 1. Arrangement Participants

Institutions authorized to operate by the Central Bank (BCB) may join the instant payment
arrangement as a financial institution, a payment institution, or an electronic money
institution, provided they are registered in the Transactional Accounts Identifiers
Directory (DICT).

## 2. Transaction Limits

For individual (retail) accounts, the default nighttime transaction limit (8 PM to 6 AM) is
R$ 1,000.00 per transaction, which the user may adjust within the participating
institution's app. During daytime hours, each participating institution must publish its
own limits, respecting the arrangement's minimum floor of R$ 10,000.00 per transaction.

## 3. Settlement

Transactions are settled in real time, irrevocably and unconditionally, through the Instant
Payment System (SPI) operated by the BCB. The maximum confirmation time to the paying user
is 10 seconds.

## 4. Fund Returns (Special Return Mechanism)

In cases of proven fraud or operational failure, the receiving user's institution may
place a precautionary block on the funds for up to 7 calendar days, by opening a Special
Return Mechanism procedure, for subsequent return to the paying user.

## 5. Fees

Charging fees to individuals for payments and transfers through the instant arrangement is
prohibited. For legal entities (businesses), charging fees is allowed, provided it is
disclosed in advance and transparently to the receiving user.
